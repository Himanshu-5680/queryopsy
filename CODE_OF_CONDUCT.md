# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Queryopsy to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or level of SQL. We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, friendly and healthy community.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with beginners.
* Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
* Focus on what is best for the project and its learners.
* Credit other people's work and respect data licences.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments of any kind.
* Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
* Publishing someone's private information without permission.
* Sharing level solutions in issues meant for discussion of bugs (use spoiler tags in Discussions instead).

## Enforcement

Report problems privately to the maintainer through GitHub (a private security advisory or a direct message to @Himanshu-5680). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and temporarily or permanently ban contributors who break these rules.
