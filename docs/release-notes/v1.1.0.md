## Queryopsy v1.1.0 - App edition

On your phone Queryopsy now feels like a real app instead of a website: large titles, a bottom tab bar, bottom sheets, swipe-back and system dark mode. The desktop website keeps the case-file look.

**Play:** https://himanshu-5680.github.io/queryopsy/ (works offline after the first visit; add it to your home screen for the app experience)

### Downloads
- `Queryopsy-v1.1.0.apk`: Android 7.0+, no permissions required. It is versionCode 3, so it upgrades v1.0.x in place and keeps your progress. To sideload it, enable "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager.
- `queryopsy-web-v1.1.0.zip`: the full offline website. Unzip it and run `python -m http.server` inside the folder (see HOW-TO-RUN.txt).

### New: app mode
- A **Cases** home with your progress ring, a Continue card and grouped case files. The app opens straight into it.
- Large titles that shrink as you scroll, a translucent navigation bar and a back chevron.
- A tab bar for **Cases, Investigate, Evidence and Progress**.
- Slide transitions. Swipe from the left edge or press Android Back to go back one step.
- Evidence tables, hints and query history open in **bottom sheets** you can drag down.
- A SQL key row with a Run button above the keyboard.
- Appearance follows your system light/dark setting, or choose Light or Dark under Progress. Haptic ticks on Android.
- On Android the app draws edge to edge, with status and navigation bars that match the theme.

### Fixed
- Phones no longer scroll sideways on the home page: the long "Continue: ..." button and the header now fit the screen.
